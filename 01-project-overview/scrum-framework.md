# Scrum Framework

Scrum organizes Ustaad.af verification and improvement into short iterations with a Product Backlog, Sprint Backlogs and inspectable increments. Existing features are the baseline, not newly completed sprint output.

| Sprint | Dates | Calendar duration | Planned goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | October 6–11, 2026 | 6 | Verify requirements, architecture, public discovery, authentication and worker profiles; establish Scrum documentation. |
| 2 | October 12–17, 2026 | 6 | Verify the complete job, application and worker-selection workflow and resolve evidenced defects. |
| 3 | October 18–23, 2026 | 6 | Verify private communication, completion, reviews and trust controls with security checks. |
| 4 | October 24–30, 2026 | 7 | Verify administration, integrate workflows, refine UX, finalize models and prepare submission evidence. |


Planning proposes a goal, ordered stories, capacity and tasks; the real meeting records the final commitment. Daily stand-ups inspect progress and blockers toward the Sprint Goal. Review demonstrates evidenced work and obtains Bahara's actual acceptance/feedback. Retrospective examines teamwork and selects actual improvements after review.

The plan treats all dates within sprint ranges as academic working days, including weekends: 25 daily stand-ups. This is a scheduling assumption to confirm, not attendance evidence. Planning precedes the first stand-up; final-day stand-up precedes review, followed by retrospective. Times remain to be confirmed in Asia/Kabul. Planning: proposed 45 minutes; daily: 15 minutes; review: 45 minutes; retrospective: 30 minutes. Capacity is not derived from these ceremony durations.

Bahara represents product needs and orders/accepts work. Ahmad facilitates events, progress transparency and evidence collection. Sahar leads implementation, tests and modeling. All three collaborate where useful. See [responsibilities](../02-team/role-responsibilities.md).

October 31 is repository review, documentation cleanup, evidence checking and submission preparation; it is outside Sprint 4. Final deadline: November 1, 2026 at 11:59 PM (schedule uses Asia/Kabul; confirm submission-system timezone). No extra meeting is scheduled for October 31.
