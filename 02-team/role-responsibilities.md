# Role Responsibilities

## Product Owner — Bahara Noorzai

Maintain/order Product Backlog; prioritize stories; define acceptance criteria; represent product needs; participate in planning; lead product feedback in reviews; accept or reject demonstrated work.

## Scrum Master — Ahmad Khalid Safi

Facilitate Scrum events/stand-ups; prepare agendas; maintain process; help remove blockers; monitor progress; assist stories; ensure documentation, minutes and recording links are captured.

## Developer — Sahar Haidary

Lead development and technical verification; contribute requirements/modeling; implement evidenced fixes; test; maintain technical documentation and meaningful GitHub commits.

## Collaboration and accountability

Bahara owns product ordering and acceptance decisions. Ahmad owns ceremony preparation and process evidence. Sahar leads technical artifacts and verification. Members can assist each other; record actual task ownership, reviewers and commits during the sprint rather than asserting past contributions. Record unresolved blockers and commitments only when they actually arise.
